How we operate

Clear steps from first contact to completed work.

01

Reach out

Call or text 978-382-3166 with a brief description of the plumbing issue. We will discuss your situation and the best next steps.

02

Schedule a visit

We arrange a day and time that works for you, with prompt service and clear communication throughout the process.

03

On-site evaluation

We assess the issue, answer your questions, and provide recommendations specific to your home or business.

An $89 service call and evaluation fee applies. If we move forward with repair or installation during the same visit, the fee is applied toward the work performed.

04

Upfront pricing

Before work begins, you receive clear pricing and recommendations based on your specific plumbing needs.

05

Quality workmanship

Once approved, we complete the work using quality materials and professional installation practices while treating your property with care.

About SABA Plumbing & Heating

Respect the property. Do the job right.

At SABA Plumbing & Heating, every project starts with a simple goal: respect your home or business and do the job the right way. Whether it is a small repair or a larger installation, we focus on clear communication, dependable workmanship, and solutions that last.

SABA Plumbing & Heating is locally owned and operated by Master Plumber Zach Saba. Zach also teaches part time in the evening as an adult education instructor at Whittier Regional Technical Vocational High School. He holds Journeyman and Master Plumber licenses, a business license, EPA certification, and is fully insured.

We work closely with homeowners, builders, and contractors so each project is handled professionally from start to finish.
